Capabilities

Designed to stay out of your way

Sub-second transcription

Streams audio to Deepgram Nova-3 for instant captions, or runs locally offline with Whisper.

Echo suppression

Automatically gates your microphone while computer audio plays so speaker bleed never clutters the transcript.

Natural auto-replies

Detects questions from the other party and streams concise, first-person answers grounded in your CV.

Screen awareness

Press Ctrl+Enter anytime to capture your active display and ask Claude about code or slides on screen.

Invisible on screen share

Protected at the OS window level so your overlay stays hidden during Zoom, Teams, Meet, and recordings.

Modes & Markdown history

Switch between Interview, Meeting, Coding, or custom modes and export any session to Markdown.

Quick Start

Ready in under a minute

01

Install

Download and run the Windows installer. Your keys and sessions stay strictly local on your PC.

02

Connect keys

Open Settings and paste your Anthropic API key and Deepgram key (encrypted with Windows DPAPI).

03

Start listening

Optionally upload your CV in Profile, choose Interview mode, and press Ctrl+Shift+L.
